E-004 ■ COMPLETED

Rehearsal probe on LongRun bigworld (seed 0, a query every 40 steps): record which concepts every view shows; for the gold endpoints that focus queries miss and that were pruned, measure how long before their pruning they were last shown.在 LongRun bigworld（种子 0，每 40 步一次查询）上做复述探针：记录每个视图展示了哪些概念；对焦点查询未到达且已被剪除的金标准端点，测量它们在被剪除前多久最后一次被展示。

Result.结果。 Of 101 pruned, unreached gold endpoints, 65 never appeared in any of 115 views (mean 23.6 concepts per view); of the 36 that did, 6 were last shown < 500 steps earlier, 20 at 500–999 and 10 at ≥ 2 000. Rehearsal could save at most about 10–15 of 121 unreached endpoints (≈ 1 in 10); effect on the bigworld composite ≤ 0.01. It would also make projection impure.在 101 个已被剪除、未被到达的金标准端点中，65 个从未出现在 115 个视图的任何一个里（每个视图平均 23.6 个概念）；出现过的 36 个中，上次出现距今 < 500 步的 6 个、500–999 步的 20 个、≥ 2 000 步的 10 个。复述最多能救回 121 个未到达端点中的约 10–15 个（≈ 十分之一）；对 bigworld 综合分的影响 ≤ 0.01。它还会使投影不再是纯函数。
