1Executive Summary执行摘要

Thirteen 2026 papers and three surveys on agent memory consolidation, forgetting, spreading activation and contradiction resolution were found by web search. Independent systems converge on World0's primitives: Hebbian co-activation, spreading activation with decay, and deterministic claim resolution.通过网页搜索找到 13 篇 2026 年论文和 3 篇综述，主题是智能体记忆巩固、遗忘、扩散激活与矛盾裁决。多个独立系统趋同于 World0 的原语：Hebbian 共同激活、带衰减的扩散激活、确定性的主张裁决。

Two borrowable mechanisms were tested directly on World0 and refuted: rehearsal on read (from retrieval-driven reconsolidation) and suppressing co-occurrence edges. One change shipped: claims and concept cards now expose their system time (Claim.since_tick / until_tick, ConceptCard.first_seen_tick).两个可借用的机制直接在 World0 上做了测试并被否定：读取即复述（来自检索驱动再巩固）与压制共现边。发布了一项改动：主张与概念卡现在暴露其系统时间（Claim.since_tick / until_tick、ConceptCard.first_seen_tick）。

E-004 ■ COMPLETED

Rehearsal probe on LongRun bigworld (seed 0, a query every 40 steps): record which concepts every view shows; for the gold endpoints that focus queries miss and that were pruned, measure how long before their pruning they were last shown.在 LongRun bigworld（种子 0，每 40 步一次查询）上做复述探针：记录每个视图展示了哪些概念；对焦点查询未到达且已被剪除的金标准端点，测量它们在被剪除前多久最后一次被展示。

Result.结果。 Of 101 pruned, unreached gold endpoints, 65 never appeared in any of 115 views (mean 23.6 concepts per view); of the 36 that did, 6 were last shown < 500 steps earlier, 20 at 500–999 and 10 at ≥ 2 000. Rehearsal could save at most about 10–15 of 121 unreached endpoints (≈ 1 in 10); effect on the bigworld composite ≤ 0.01. It would also make projection impure.在 101 个已被剪除、未被到达的金标准端点中，65 个从未出现在 115 个视图的任何一个里（每个视图平均 23.6 个概念）；出现过的 36 个中，上次出现距今 < 500 步的 6 个、500–999 步的 20 个、≥ 2 000 步的 10 个。复述最多能救回 121 个未到达端点中的约 10–15 个（≈ 十分之一）；对 bigworld 综合分的影响 ≤ 0.01。它还会使投影不再是纯函数。

7Engineering Recommendations工程建议

  • Keep projection a pure function: do not add rehearsal on read (H-004 refuted).保持投影为纯函数：不加入“读取即复述”（H-004 已否定）。
  • Keep the co-occurrence edge weight: suppressing it trades closure for precision (H-005 refuted).保持共现边权重不变：压制它是用闭包换精确率（H-005 已否定）。
  • Expose claim system time in the API: shipped as optional fields, wire format world0/1 unchanged.在接口中暴露主张的系统时间：已作为可选字段发布，线格式 world0/1 不变。
  • Defer hub-to-summary condensation (HeLa-Mem): it leans toward summary storage and needs a design discussion first.暂缓“枢纽凝成摘要”（HeLa-Mem）：它偏向摘要存储，需要先做设计讨论。
