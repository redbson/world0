Core thesis核心论点

Memory should be repaired offline before retrieval fails, via self-probing dream cycles.应在检索失败之前，通过自我探测的梦循环离线修复记忆。

Mechanism机制

Offline dream cycles with three agents (Dreamer, Analyst, Consolidator) that probe, diagnose and repair a memory graph; policy trained with GRPO; soft decay gives reversible forgetting; online interaction continues during consolidation.离线梦循环由三个智能体（Dreamer、Analyst、Consolidator）对记忆图进行探测、诊断与修复；策略用 GRPO 训练；软衰减实现可逆遗忘；巩固期间在线交互不中断。
