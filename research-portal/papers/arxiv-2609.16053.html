Agent interpretation研究代理解读
Rewriting memories when they are read conflicts with World0's pure projection (reads do not change state, Corollary 3.2'). The borrowable form, rehearsal (reads refresh recency), was tested and refuted (H-004).读取时改写记忆与 World0 的纯函数投影冲突（读取不改变状态，推论 3.2′）。可借用的形式——复述（读取刷新新近度）——已经测试并被否定（H-004）。
Speculative hypotheses推测性假设
Retrieval feedback might still be usable as an explicit, separate observation event rather than a side effect of reading (see H-003).检索反馈或许仍可作为一个显式、独立的观察事件来使用，而不是读取的副作用（见 H-003）。
