Core thesis核心论点

Ontology can act as a kernel for agents: task-oriented ontologies and knowledge graphs are constructed dynamically and refined using judge feedback (OaK).本体可作为智能体的内核：面向任务的本体与知识图谱被动态构建，并依据评判反馈持续精化（OaK）。
