Experimental observations
World0 probe E-004 (bigworld seed 0): of 101 pruned gold endpoints that focus queries missed, 65 had never appeared in any view; rehearsal on read could save at most about one in ten.
Agent interpretation
Rewriting memories when they are read conflicts with World0's pure projection (reads do not change state, Corollary 3.2'). The borrowable form, rehearsal (reads refresh recency), was tested and refuted (H-004).
Speculative hypotheses
Retrieval feedback might still be usable as an explicit, separate observation event rather than a side effect of reading (see H-003).
