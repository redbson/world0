1Executive Summary

Thirteen 2026 papers and three surveys on agent memory consolidation, forgetting, spreading activation and contradiction resolution were found by web search. Independent systems converge on World0's primitives: Hebbian co-activation, spreading activation with decay, and deterministic claim resolution.

Two borrowable mechanisms were tested directly on World0 and refuted: rehearsal on read (from retrieval-driven reconsolidation) and suppressing co-occurrence edges. One change shipped: claims and concept cards now expose their system time (Claim.since_tick / until_tick, ConceptCard.first_seen_tick).

Recommended action

6New Research Hypotheses

H-004✕ REFUTED

Rehearsal (a read refreshes the recency of what it shows), borrowed from retrieval-driven reconsolidation, would recover a meaningful share of the true endpoints World0 forgets on bigworld.

Motivation
Retrieval-Driven Memory Reconsolidation rewrites memories when they are retrieved; rehearsal is the form that fits World0.
Supporting evidence
  • arxiv:2609.16053
  • E-004: 65 of 101 forgotten endpoints were never shown; at most about 1 in 10 recoverable

Experiments and status history

E-004 ■ COMPLETED

Rehearsal probe on LongRun bigworld (seed 0, a query every 40 steps): record which concepts every view shows; for the gold endpoints that focus queries miss and that were pruned, measure how long before their pruning they were last shown.

Result. Of 101 pruned, unreached gold endpoints, 65 never appeared in any of 115 views (mean 23.6 concepts per view); of the 36 that did, 6 were last shown < 500 steps earlier, 20 at 500–999 and 10 at ≥ 2 000. Rehearsal could save at most about 10–15 of 121 unreached endpoints (≈ 1 in 10); effect on the bigworld composite ≤ 0.01. It would also make projection impure.

Artifact: Probe script kept in the session scratchpad (not committed); numbers in docs/research/notes/2026-10-08.md on dev.

H-005✕ REFUTED

Suppressing co-occurrence (generic) edges in activation improves projection quality by raising focus precision (0.60) without losing recall or closure.

Motivation
Co-occurrence edges might carry activation too far from the task's context.
Supporting evidence

E-005 ■ COMPLETED

Ablate the weight of co-occurrence edges in activation on LongRun main (seeds 0–2, 1 200-token budget): default vs × 0.5 vs 0.

Result. Default: composite 0.988, focus recall 0.988, chain 0.964, focus precision 0.604, 604 tokens. × 0.5: identical. Weight 0: composite 0.978, recall 0.979, chain 0.932, precision 0.640, 556 tokens. Co-occurrence edges supply reachability when explicit paths are missing; only their presence matters.

7Engineering Recommendations

  • Keep projection a pure function: do not add rehearsal on read (H-004 refuted).
  • Keep the co-occurrence edge weight: suppressing it trades closure for precision (H-005 refuted).
  • Expose claim system time in the API: shipped as optional fields, wire format world0/1 unchanged.
  • Defer hub-to-summary condensation (HeLa-Mem): it leans toward summary storage and needs a design discussion first.
